# Philosophy

> *"Understanding precedes prediction. Prediction precedes preparation. Preparation protects the future."*

## Why I Started

I did not begin this journey to build another piece of software.

I began because I believe humanity faces a fundamental challenge: we are forced to defend against futures we do not yet understand.

Cybersecurity has long been reactive. We investigate yesterday's attacks, patch yesterday's vulnerabilities, and prepare for yesterday's adversaries. Meanwhile, technology continues to evolve, incentives continue to change, and new forms of conflict emerge.

I believe there is another path.

One built upon understanding rather than reaction.

---

## First Principles

- Every attack is the consequence of a decision.
- Every decision is shaped by incentives.
- Every incentive exists within an evolving technological landscape.
- To anticipate the future, we must understand the forces that create it.

---

## Understanding Over Recognition

I measure progress not by recognition, but by understanding.

Every insight matters.

Every failure teaches.

Every question is an opportunity to learn.

Every day is another step toward a future humanity does not yet know how to see.

---

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence should not be judged solely by what it can accomplish.

It should also be judged by:

- How well we understand it.
- How safely we can control it.
- How transparently it reasons.
- How responsibly it is developed.

Capability without understanding creates uncertainty.

Understanding without capability creates opportunity.

Humanity must pursue both.

---

## Research

I believe questions are more valuable than assumptions.

A good question can define years of research.

A great question can define a lifetime.

---

## My Mission

My mission is not to predict every attack.

My mission is to understand the evolution of cyberspace deeply enough that humanity can prepare for the future before it arrives.

Everything I build serves that purpose.
