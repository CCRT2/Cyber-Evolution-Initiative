# 🎙️ Podcasts

## 1. Darknet Diaries

**Hosted by:** Jack Rhysider

One of the most popular cybersecurity podcasts, featuring real stories about hackers, cybercrime, penetration testing, and digital investigations.

---

## 2. CyberWire Daily

**Hosted by:** Dave Bittner

A daily cybersecurity news podcast covering the latest threats, vulnerabilities, and industry developments in an easy-to-follow format.

---

## 3. Risky Business

**Hosted by:** Patrick Gray

A weekly podcast offering in-depth discussions on cybersecurity news, enterprise security, and emerging threats with expert guests.

---

## 4. Click Here

**Hosted by:** Dina Temple-Raston

Explores the human side of cybersecurity through stories about cybercrime, espionage, misinformation, and digital privacy.

---

## 5. Smashing Security

**Hosted by:** Graham Cluley & Carole Theriault

A fun and engaging podcast covering cybersecurity news, scams, online privacy, and security with expert analysis and humor.
