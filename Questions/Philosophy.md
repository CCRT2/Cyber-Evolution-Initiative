# Philosophy Questions

Questions about knowledge, reality, intelligence, prediction, understanding, and humanity's relationship with technology.

- What does it mean to truly understand something?
- Is prediction a form of understanding?
- Can the future be fundamentally unpredictable, or only difficult to model?
- What is intelligence?
- What is consciousness?
- Is intelligence independent of the physical substrate that produces it?
- Are humans discovering the laws of intelligence or creating new forms of it?
- Can a system understand itself?
- What separates knowledge from understanding?
- What responsibilities arise when humanity creates systems more capable than their creators in specific domains?
- How should humanity balance technological progress with uncertainty about its consequences?
- Can there be a science of intelligence that is as rigorous as physics or biology?
