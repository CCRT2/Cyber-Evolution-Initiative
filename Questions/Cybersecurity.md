# Cybersecurity Questions

Questions about the evolution, behavior, incentives, and future of cyberspace.

- Can humanity understand the evolution of cyberspace well enough to anticipate the future instead of merely reacting to it?
- Can future classes of cyberattacks be predicted before they exist in the wild?
- What forces drive the evolution of cyberspace?
- Why do cyberattacks happen?
- Can attacker incentives be modeled mathematically?
- How do technological changes create new attack opportunities?
- What signals appear before an emerging attack technique becomes widespread?
- Can AI identify the conditions that make a new attack pathway likely?
- Can defensive systems anticipate entirely new attack classes?
- How does cybersecurity evolve in response to changes in technology, economics, geopolitics, and human behavior?
- Can cyber conflict be modeled as an evolving complex system?
