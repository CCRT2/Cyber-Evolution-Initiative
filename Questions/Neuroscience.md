# Neuroscience Questions

Questions about biological intelligence, brains, learning, memory, neural systems, and consciousness.

- How does the brain produce intelligence from biological processes?
- What role do neural connectivity and network architecture play in cognition?
- How does the brain learn continuously without traditional batch training?
- How do memory, attention, emotion, and perception interact?
- What mechanisms allow the brain to form and maintain a model of itself?
- What biological processes are necessary for consciousness?
- Can the essential computational properties of biological intelligence be reproduced artificially?
- Which properties of brains are fundamental to intelligence, and which are adaptations to biology?
- How does the brain balance exploration, learning, prediction, and survival?
- Could neuroscience reveal principles that lead to fundamentally new AI architectures?
