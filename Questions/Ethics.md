# Ethics Questions

Questions about responsibility, safety, control, governance, and the consequences of creating increasingly capable technologies.

- What responsibilities do creators have when they cannot fully predict how a system will behave?
- How should society evaluate technologies whose capabilities evolve faster than our understanding of them?
- How much capability should be developed before adequate control mechanisms exist?
- What does meaningful control over an autonomous intelligent system actually mean?
- How can AI evaluations measure dangerous emergent behavior before deployment?
- How should researchers balance scientific openness with the risks of releasing powerful capabilities?
- Who is responsible when an autonomous system produces an unforeseen harmful outcome?
- Can technological progress be accelerated without allowing safety and understanding to fall behind?
- What principles should govern the development of systems capable of affecting critical infrastructure or human safety?
- How should humanity decide when a technology is too uncertain to deploy?
