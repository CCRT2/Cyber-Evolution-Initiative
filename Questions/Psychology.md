# Psychology Questions

Questions about human behavior, decision-making, motivation, cognition, and the factors that shape behavior in cyberspace.

- Why do people make harmful decisions when they understand the risks?
- How do incentives, fear, status, curiosity, ideology, and survival shape decisions?
- Can human decision-making be modeled well enough to predict behavior?
- How does cognitive bias affect cybersecurity decisions?
- How do attackers rationalize their decisions?
- How do defenders behave under uncertainty and pressure?
- How does group behavior change individual decision-making?
- Can psychological signals help identify emerging cyber threats?
- How might autonomous AI agents develop behaviors analogous to human incentives?
- What aspects of human cognition are essential to intelligence?
