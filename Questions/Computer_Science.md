# Computer Science Questions

Questions about computation, software, operating systems, networks, distributed systems, and the foundations of computing.

- What are the fundamental limits of computation?
- Which properties of computing systems enable complex emergent behavior?
- How do operating systems shape the capabilities and attack surface of modern systems?
- How do distributed systems behave when their components have competing objectives?
- Can we formally model the evolution of software ecosystems?
- How does software complexity create unforeseen vulnerabilities?
- What patterns cause previously unrelated technologies to become tightly coupled?
- Can the future behavior of large software ecosystems be predicted?
- How can secure system design account for capabilities that emerge through composition?
- What forms of computation are most useful for modeling intelligence and cyberspace?
