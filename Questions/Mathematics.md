# Mathematics Questions

Questions about mathematics as a language for understanding intelligence, uncertainty, prediction, and complex systems.

- What mathematical structures best describe intelligence?
- Can complex behavior be predicted from relatively simple mathematical rules?
- What makes a system mathematically predictable or unpredictable?
- How can probability represent uncertainty about future cyber events?
- Can attacker incentives be represented as mathematical models?
- What mathematical tools are most useful for modeling technological evolution?
- Can information theory help quantify intelligence or understanding?
- What is the mathematical boundary between prediction and randomness?
- Can mathematical models identify leading indicators of emerging cyber threats?
- How can mathematical models be tested against real-world observations without overfitting history?
