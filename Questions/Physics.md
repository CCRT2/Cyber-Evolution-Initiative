# Physics Questions

Questions about physical reality, information, energy, computation, and the boundaries of what can be modeled.

- What physical limits constrain computation and intelligence?
- Is information fundamentally physical?
- Are there physical limits to prediction?
- What role does energy efficiency play in biological and artificial intelligence?
- Could new computing substrates enable forms of intelligence unavailable to conventional computers?
- What can physics tell us about the relationship between information and complexity?
- Are there fundamental limits to simulating a complex physical system?
- How do physical constraints shape the evolution of technological systems?
