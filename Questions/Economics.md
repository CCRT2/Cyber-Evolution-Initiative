# Economics Questions

Questions about incentives, markets, resources, competition, and how economic forces shape technology and cyber behavior.

- How do economic incentives influence cybercrime?
- How do the costs and benefits of an attack affect attacker decisions?
- Can changes in economic conditions predict changes in cyber threat activity?
- How do markets accelerate or constrain technological evolution?
- How do financial incentives shape the development of AI capabilities?
- Can economic models predict when new technologies will be adopted or abused?
- How do organizations make security decisions under uncertainty?
- What happens when the economic incentives of attackers and defenders become asymmetric?
- Can game theory model long-term interactions between attackers, defenders, and autonomous systems?
- How do resource constraints change the behavior of intelligent agents?
