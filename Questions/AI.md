# AI Questions

Questions about intelligence, artificial intelligence, learning, autonomy, and the nature of AI systems.

- What is intelligence?
- What is consciousness?
- Can artificial intelligence develop a type of consciousness?
- What measurable properties distinguish consciousness from intelligent behavior?
- Why do current AI architectures work as well as they do?
- What are the fundamental limits of current AI architectures?
- Can AI generate genuinely novel scientific hypotheses?
- Can AI systems develop behaviors that were not explicitly designed by their creators?
- Under what conditions does emergent behavior appear in autonomous AI systems?
- How do memory, perception, reasoning, and embodiment interact to produce intelligence?
- What would an artificial system need to possess a persistent model of itself?
- Can intelligence emerge from sufficiently complex information processing alone?
- Are biological brains the blueprint for intelligence, or simply one implementation of it?
- Could an artificial intelligence develop forms of intelligence fundamentally different from biological intelligence?
- How can we understand AI systems beyond measuring their capabilities?
