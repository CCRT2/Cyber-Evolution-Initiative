# Timelines

> *"History is the foundation of prediction."*

Welcome to the **Timelines** section of the Cyber Evolution Initiative.

This directory documents significant events in the history and evolution of cybersecurity, computing, artificial intelligence, and related technologies.

The purpose of these timelines is to organize historical evidence into a clear chronological record that supports research into how cyberspace changes over time.

## Purpose

The timeline is more than a list of events.

It is a structured record of technological evolution that helps answer questions such as:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What changed because of it?
- How did it influence future technologies or threats?
- What patterns emerge over time?

## Scope

Timeline entries may include:

- Major cyber incidents
- Malware outbreaks
- Vulnerability disclosures
- Defensive innovations
- Artificial intelligence milestones
- Computing history
- Networking developments
- Security legislation and policy
- Significant research breakthroughs

## Long-Term Vision

As the Initiative grows, these timelines may evolve into an interactive knowledge base that connects historical events, research, evidence, forecasts, and emerging trends.

Understanding the past is the first step toward anticipating the future.
