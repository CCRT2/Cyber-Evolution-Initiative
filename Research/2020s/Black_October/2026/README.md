# Black October 2026

Working index for significant cybersecurity incidents reported during October 2026.

## Incident register

Add one entry per incident, recording:
- Date discovered or reported
- Organization, sector, and geographic scope
- Incident category
- What is confirmed versus unverified
- Operational or data impact, where established
- Primary sources and publication dates
- CEI assessment and confidence level

## Research notes

Keep this collection focused on cyber incidents. Non-malicious service outages belong in [Infrastructure Incidents](../../Infrastructure_Incidents/README.md), with cross-references here only when relevant.
