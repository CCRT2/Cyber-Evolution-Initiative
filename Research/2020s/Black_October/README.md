# Black October

Annual CEI research collection documenting significant cybersecurity incidents reported during October.

## Scope
- Record incidents with dates, affected sectors, and reliable sources.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from allegations and uncertain reporting.
- Categorize incidents by event type, such as ransomware, vulnerability exploitation, data exposure, and infrastructure disruption.
- Do not assume October is statistically the most active month without multi-year comparative data.
