# Infrastructure Incidents

CEI research on major failures and disruptions affecting digital infrastructure and online services, including incidents caused by software defects, configuration errors, capacity constraints, and cascading dependencies.

## Documentation principles
- Do not classify an outage as a cyberattack without evidence of malicious activity.
- Record the affected services, timeline, scope, root cause, and recovery actions.
- Separate the provider's confirmed findings from independent reporting and CEI analysis.
- Track uncertainty and cite primary sources wherever possible.
