# Questions

> "A question is the beginning of understanding."

This document contains the questions that define my life's work. Some may take days to answer. Others may take decades. Many may never have a complete answer. That is okay. The purpose of this document is not to collect answers, but to guide the pursuit of understanding.

## Cyber Evolution

- Can humanity understand the evolution of cyberspace well enough to anticipate the future instead of merely reacting to it?
- Can future classes of cyberattacks be predicted before they exist in the wild?
- What forces drive the evolution of cyberspace?
- Can attacker incentives be modeled mathematically?
- Can AI reason about technological evolution instead of historical events?
- What is the minimum amount of information required to predict an emerging threat?
- Can defensive systems anticipate entirely new attack classes?

## Artificial Intelligence

- What is intelligence?
- What is consciousness?
- Can artificial intelligence develop a type of consciousness?
- What measurable properties distinguish consciousness from intelligent behavior?
- Can AI generate genuinely novel scientific hypotheses?
- What are the limits of autonomous reasoning?

## Complex Systems

- How do incentives shape technological evolution?
- How do complex systems evolve?
- Can the future of a complex system be predicted?
- What causes emergent behavior?
- Under what conditions do autonomous agents organize into coordinated systems?

## Philosophy

- What does it mean to understand something?
- Is prediction a form of understanding?
- Can every decision be traced back to incentives?
- Is the future fundamentally unpredictable, or merely difficult to understand?
